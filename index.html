Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

What happens during a clarity call?

+

We talk — honestly and without an agenda beyond understanding where you are. By the end, you'll have language for what you're experiencing and a sense of what a helpful next step could look like.

Is coaching right for me?

+

If you feel a persistent gap between how your life looks and how it actually feels, coaching is likely to help. It's especially suited to women navigating identity, confidence, or a sense of being stretched too thin.

What can I bring to a coaching session?

+

Nothing but yourself and whatever is on your mind. You don't need to arrive with the "right" problem or a polished way of explaining it.

How many sessions will I need?

+

That depends on what you're working through. Some women find real clarity in a single session; others prefer an ongoing rhythm. We'll figure out what's right for you together.

Is this therapy?

+

No. Coaching is forward-facing — it focuses on clarity, mindset and next steps rather than clinical treatment. If therapy would serve you better, I'll say so honestly.

How do I book a session?

+

Use the "Book a Clarity Call" button anywhere on this page, or message me directly on Instagram — I read every message myself.
